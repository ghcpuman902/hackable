Notes for the builder

  • Every file is self-contained. The shared tokens block at the top of each <style> is identical; lift it into globals.
  • The grey top bar (.mock-nav) is for clicking between mockups. Drop it in the app.
  • 01 passes chips to 02 as ?q= JSON. In the app, send them to the agent route instead.
  • 02 is a scripted timeline. In the app, stream the same event types: src, feed, trait, who, pat, done.
  • Screenshot helpers: 02-progress.html?speed=50, 03-*.html?scene=3.
  • 01 and 04 follow the Quintex inline-icon text style (S-quintex/qag/reports/hack-style-brief.md): Inter 600, -0.025em, 22px/1.5 then 28px/1.45 from 640px, dark lead #171717 then grey #737373, white lucide glyphs (stroke 2.5) in solid 0.95em tiles. Icons are inlined SVG, no network.
  • All data is synthetic. Live demo subjects: Mangle, or an organiser who has agreed on the day.
