How it works

  1. Malware on your computer copies your browser's saved sessions.
  2. Those sessions get sold in a “stealer log”.
  3. A buyer loads them and opens your accounts as you.
  4. Password and two-step codes are skipped. You are already “signed in”.

Signs

  • Your email shows up in an infostealer check, like this one.
  • New devices or sessions in account settings you don't recognise.

What to do

  • Clean or reset the infected computer first.
  • Sign out of all sessions everywhere, then change passwords.
  • Use passkeys where you can. Tell work if work tools were on it.
