How it works

  1. Someone logs in to your account with a leaked password.
  2. They buy something or change your details. You get an alert.
  3. Seconds later, a bot signs your email up to hundreds of sites.
  4. The alert is buried. By the time you find it, it's too late.

Signs

  • A sudden flood of “Welcome” and “Confirm your email” messages.
  • They are from real sites you never visited.

What to do

  • Don't just delete. Search the flood for orders, logins and password resets.
  • Check your bank and shopping accounts straight away.
  • Change the leaked passwords and turn on two-step sign-in.
