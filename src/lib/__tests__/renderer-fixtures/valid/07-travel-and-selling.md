**Travel and marketplace leaks put you on scammers' lists.**

The email for Ravi Menon appears in breaches at a booking site and a selling app. Those lists are used for fake booking and fake buyer messages.

> [!CAUTION]
> **A fake booking message could ask you to pay again.** It looks like your hotel or airline and links to a copy site.[^1]

> [!CAUTION]
> **A fake buyer may ask you to use their courier link.** The link asks for your card details to receive payment.[^2]

> [!TIP]
> **Only pay or get paid inside the official app or site.** Ignore links sent in messages.
