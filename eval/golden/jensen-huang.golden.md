# Report

**You are The Headline: this week, almost every story about AI has your name in it.**

NVIDIA [reinstated you as chief executive](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) on 23 November 2026, and you are [due before three House committees](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/jensen-huang-nvidia-congress-gtc.html) on 1 December. NVIDIA's own statement mentions reporting that you were the target of a foreign intelligence effort.

**Big news is the best bait, and state backed groups know how to use it.**

Phishing dressed up as a news story and [tailored phishing from state groups](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa23-341a) both feed on weeks like this. Your work address is easy to guess too, because [NVIDIA uses first initial plus surname](https://www.clay.com/dossier/nvidia-email-format).

**The one thing to learn: treat any urgent message about the hearings as fake until you check it another way.**

Don't click the link or reply. Contact the sender through a number or address you already trust. The NCSC explains how in [Phishing: how to spot and report it](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/suspicious-email-actions).
